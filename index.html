About Us

At Karnan Holidays, we specialize in crafting memorable journeys from Tamil Nadu to Kashmir. We offer a wide range of tour packages including Family, Bachelor, Corporate, and Customized trips, designed to suit every traveler. With a focus on comfort, safety, and affordability, we ensure each destination is handpicked for the best experience. From southern temples to northern snow peaks, we bring India closer to you. We don’t just plan trips, we create lasting memories.
